Hi, I'm Aleksandra.

Hi, I'm Aleksandra.

I love photographing in a way that makes every picture not just an image, but a story.

I believe every moment can be made special, and my camera just helps highlight it.

And yes, don’t be surprised if you see me up a tree or in the bushes – I can be craizy in pursuit of the perfect shot!😄

What am I photographing?

I love photographing everything I see around me: nature, architecture, people, and capturing the unnoticed moments of social events and celebrations. I enjoy spending a long time perfecting a single posed shot, as well as disappearing from view during documentary shooting at events.
This all makes up a significant part of my hobby called photography. I would love to combine all these aspects in my profession, but with experience, I have decided to focus on the main areas where I can fully invest myself and bring people more joy and satisfaction from enriching their archives with my photos and stories.
